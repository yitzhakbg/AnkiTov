1//! Management Console controllers module.
2//!
3//! Exposes 10 sub-controllers:
4//! - `decks` — Deck upload, distribution, and listing
5//! - `stats` — Whole-class and subsection statistics
6//! - `addons` — Addon upload, distribution, and configuration
7//! - `sync` — Sync server control and user status
8//! - `anki_ops` — Zone 1 + Zone 2 + Zone 3 Anki operations (no MCP server)
9//! - `probe` — AnkiConnect health probe
10//! - `tracks` — Track Library CRUD (Phase 2)
11//! - `track_profiles` — Track Profile assignment (Phase 2)
12//! - `capsule_sessions` — Capsule generation & listing (Phase 3)
13//! - `compliance` — Weekly & per-student compliance tracking (Phase 4)
14
